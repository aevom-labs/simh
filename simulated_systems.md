## Notes

- A `word` is the native data unit of a simulated CPU.
- While `1 byte = 8 bits` is conventional in modern sense, several historical systems were not byte-oriented in modern
  sense.
- Unit conversions: `1 KW = 1024 words`; `1 MW = 1024 KW`; `1 KB = 1024 eight-bit bytes`; `1 MB = 1024 KB`

These values are summarized from the [classic SIMH hardware list](https://simh.trailing-edge.com/hardware.html)
and the individual simulator configuration pages linked in the table. For
architecture-level distinction between native words and host bytes, refer to
the [SIMH simulator documentation](https://simh.trailing-edge.com/docs/simh.pdf).
